HR Recruitment Policy - Demonstration

1. Candidates should be evaluated against published job requirements using job-related evidence only.
2. Recruiters should see matched skills, missing skills, experience evidence, and recommendation reasons.
3. AI output is decision support only. A qualified recruiter must review the candidate before any hiring decision.
4. Do not use protected characteristics or unrelated personal attributes in candidate scoring or recommendations.
5. Interview questions must be relevant to the role and must not target protected characteristics.
6. If the resume does not provide evidence, mark it unknown or missing rather than inventing evidence.
7. The final hiring decision belongs to a human recruiter.
